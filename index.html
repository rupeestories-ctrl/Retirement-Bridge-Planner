Retirement phases

What changed in this version

  • Balances grow from current age to retirement age. Annual savings are added before retirement.
  • Rule of 55, SEPP, Roth conversions, and taxable spending can work together.
  • Pretax withdrawals are reduced by the effective tax rate entered by the user.
  • Roth contribution and conversion principal stay fixed. Market gains remain earnings. One prior conversion vintage can also be entered.
  • RMD start age comes from birth year. The model uses age 75 for people born in 1960 or later.
  • Inflation shocks carry into later years. A 25% crash is treated as a -25% stock return.
  • 529 withdrawals separate contribution basis and earnings.

Annual timing

The model runs in one-year steps. Standard age-59½ access may therefore appear in the first row at or above age 59½. Contributions are added at the end of each working year. Retirement spending starts with today's spending grown to the retirement year.
